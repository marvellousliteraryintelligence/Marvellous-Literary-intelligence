Ongoing Marketing Support

Continued strategic guidance for authors who want a knowledgeable partner beyond the initial plan. Regular check-ins, updated recommendations, and responsive advice as your book's situation and the broader market landscape evolve over time.

Book Marketing Is Not a One-Time Event

One of the most common misconceptions in book marketing is that visibility is something you achieve once and then maintain passively. In reality, the online retail environment is in constant motion. Algorithms change. New competitors enter your genre. Reader behaviour shifts. Retail platforms update their interfaces and policies. A strategy that was well-suited to your book six months ago may need adjustment today.

At the same time, your own situation as an author is also evolving. You may be writing new books, expanding into new genres, experimenting with different formats, or navigating changes in your personal capacity to devote time to marketing. Each of these shifts can alter what your book needs from a visibility standpoint.

Ongoing Marketing Support is designed for authors who recognise that book discovery is a continuous process, not a problem to be solved once and forgotten. It provides a steady, informed presence alongside your author career, helping you stay responsive to change without having to figure everything out alone.

The Problem

What Happens After the Strategy Is Delivered

Many authors complete a marketing strategy or a round of implementation and then face a familiar dilemma. The initial work is done. The recommendations have been applied. But then what?

Without ongoing guidance, several things tend to happen:

  • Drift: The careful changes you made gradually get overwritten by well-meaning but uninformed tweaks, or they simply become outdated as the retail environment shifts around them.
  • Uncertainty: When something changes in your book's performance or visibility, you are not sure whether it is a normal fluctuation, a platform update, or a signal that something needs attention.
  • Isolation: You encounter a new marketing question or opportunity and have no one to ask who understands your specific situation. Generic online advice may or may not apply to you, and you have no way to tell.
  • Missed opportunities: New promotional windows, genre trends, or platform features emerge that could benefit your book, but you are not aware of them or do not know how to evaluate their relevance.
  • Reactive scrambling: Instead of maintaining steady, informed visibility efforts, you find yourself reacting to crises or sudden drops in performance without a clear framework for understanding what happened.

Ongoing Marketing Support addresses all of these challenges by providing a consistent point of contact who knows your book, understands your genre, and can help you navigate the evolving landscape with clarity and confidence.

Scope

What Ongoing Support Includes

The structure of each ongoing support arrangement is tailored to the author's needs, but the service generally encompasses the following elements:

Regular strategic check-ins

Scheduled written consultations at an agreed frequency to review your book's current visibility, discuss any changes in the retail environment or your own situation, and identify emerging priorities. These check-ins provide a rhythm of accountability and reflection that helps you stay focused on what matters most.

Responsive advice

Access to timely guidance when questions arise between scheduled check-ins. Whether you are considering a new promotional opportunity, responding to an unexpected change in your book's performance, or evaluating a marketing service you have been approached about, you can reach out for an informed perspective.

Updated recommendations

As the market landscape shifts and your book's situation evolves, we provide refreshed recommendations to keep your visibility strategy aligned with current realities. This may include adjustments to metadata, new category opportunities, seasonal promotional ideas, or changes to your author platform approach.

Launch and release support

If you are publishing new books during the support period, we can provide strategic guidance for each release, helping you apply the lessons learned from previous launches and adapt your approach to the specific needs of each new title.

Market awareness

We keep an eye on broader trends and changes in the online book retail environment that may affect your visibility. When something significant shifts, such as a major platform update or a change in how readers discover books in your genre, we flag it and discuss what it means for your strategy.

A note on flexibility: Ongoing support is not a rigid contract. The frequency of check-ins, the scope of advice, and the focus areas can all be adjusted as your needs change. Some authors need more intensive support during a launch period and less during quieter months. We work with you to find a rhythm that makes sense for your career and your life.

Deliverables

What You Can Expect

The specific deliverables of an ongoing support arrangement depend on the agreed scope, but you can generally expect the following:

  • Scheduled written check-in reports: concise summaries of our discussions, including any recommendations, observations, or action items arising from each consultation
  • Responsive email guidance: timely written responses to your questions and concerns between scheduled check-ins, drawing on our knowledge of your book and your genre
  • Periodic visibility snapshots: brief assessments of your book's current discoverability at key intervals, noting any significant changes since the previous review
  • Strategic recommendations: updated advice as your situation and the market evolve, ensuring your approach remains relevant and effective
  • Resource and opportunity alerts: notifications about relevant promotional opportunities, platform changes, or industry developments that may affect your book's visibility

The emphasis throughout is on practical, actionable guidance rather than lengthy reports. We respect your time and focus on the information that will genuinely help you make better decisions about your book's marketing.

Our Process

How Ongoing Support Works

Ongoing support is designed to integrate smoothly into your author life without adding unnecessary complexity.

  1. Foundation review We begin by reviewing any previous work we have done together, such as a discoverability analysis, marketing strategy, or implementation project. If you are new to our services, we conduct an initial assessment to establish a baseline understanding of your book's current visibility and your goals.
  2. Support structure agreement We agree on the frequency of check-ins, the preferred method of communication, and the general scope of the support. This structure is flexible and can be adjusted at any time as your needs change.
  3. Regular check-ins At the agreed intervals, we conduct a written consultation to review your book's current situation, discuss any developments or concerns, and identify priorities for the coming period. These check-ins are focused and practical, designed to give you clear direction without consuming excessive time.
  4. Responsive support between check-ins Between scheduled consultations, you can reach out with questions, concerns, or opportunities you want to evaluate. We respond with informed, situation-specific advice rather than generic guidance.
  5. Periodic review and adjustment At regular intervals, we step back to assess the overall direction of the support arrangement. Are the check-ins at the right frequency? Is the focus still aligned with your priorities? Should the scope expand or contract? This ensures the support remains genuinely useful as your career evolves.
Who This Is For

Is This Service Right for You?

Ongoing Marketing Support is designed for authors who view book marketing as a long-term endeavour and want a knowledgeable partner to help them navigate it. It is particularly relevant if you recognise yourself in any of the following situations:

  • You have completed a strategy or implementation project with us and want to maintain momentum rather than losing focus once the initial work is done
  • You are publishing multiple books over time and want consistent strategic guidance across your growing catalogue
  • You find the pace of change in online book retail overwhelming and want someone to help you separate signal from noise
  • You have experienced the frustration of making marketing decisions in isolation and want a trusted sounding board for new ideas and opportunities
  • You are a career author who wants to invest in sustained visibility rather than short-term promotional bursts
  • You value having access to informed, situation-specific advice without the pressure of a rigid long-term commitment

This service is not limited to authors who have previously worked with us, though we find that the most effective ongoing arrangements build on a foundation of prior diagnostic or strategic work. If you are new to our services, we will discuss the best way to establish that foundation before beginning the ongoing support.

Important Limitations

What This Service Does Not Do

Ongoing support is a guidance and advisory service, not a managed marketing department. We want to be clear about what it involves and what it does not.

No guaranteed outcomes

Continued strategic guidance may help you make better marketing decisions over time, but we cannot guarantee specific improvements in sales, rankings, visibility, or any other metric. The book market is influenced by many factors beyond strategic advice.

No day-to-day management

Ongoing support provides strategic guidance and responsive advice. It does not include daily management of your social media accounts, advertising campaigns, email lists, or other marketing channels. You remain the person executing the day-to-day work unless you ask for support.

No unlimited availability

While we aim to respond to questions in a timely manner, ongoing support does not provide instant or round-the-clock access. Response times and availability are agreed at the outset of the arrangement and are designed to be sustainable for both parties.

No replacement for specialised services

Ongoing support provides general strategic guidance. If your situation requires specialised work such as a full discoverability audit, a comprehensive strategy overhaul, or hands-on implementation, we may recommend engaging one of our other services alongside the ongoing support.
