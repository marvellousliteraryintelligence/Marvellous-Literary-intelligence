Thank You for Reaching Out

Taking the step to ask why your books are not being discovered is one of the most important things you can do as an author. We appreciate you getting in touch with Marvellous Literary Intelligence.

What to Expect

If you have sent us an email, here is a general outline of what happens next.

01

We Read Your Message

Every enquiry is read carefully and individually. We want to understand your situation, your books, and the specific visibility challenges you are facing before we respond.

02

We Reply Personally

You will receive a personal response, not an automated template. We may ask a few follow-up questions to better understand your goals and the current state of your book's online presence.

03

We Explore Fit

Together, we will determine whether our services are the right match for your needs. If they are, we will discuss scope, approach, and next steps. If not, we will point you in a helpful direction.

A gentle note: Because this website uses direct email links rather than an automated form system, we cannot confirm whether your message was successfully delivered to our inbox. If you do not hear back within a few business days, please try sending your email again to MarvellousLiteraryIntelligence@gmail.com. Occasionally, messages can be delayed or filtered by email providers.
