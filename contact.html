Your First Message

What to Include in Your Enquiry

You do not need to write a formal brief or prepare a complex document. A straightforward, honest message is all we need to begin a conversation. That said, the following information helps us understand your situation more quickly and respond with more relevant guidance:

About Your Book

  • The title and genre of your book
  • When and how it was published (self-published, hybrid, traditional)
  • Which retail platforms it is available on
  • A link to your book's primary retail page, if available

About Your Situation

  • What prompted you to reach out to us
  • The specific visibility challenges you are experiencing
  • Any marketing efforts you have already tried
  • What you hope to achieve by working with a strategist

If you are not sure which of our services is right for you, do not worry. Simply describe your situation in the form above and we will recommend the most appropriate starting point. You can also browse our Services page or read through our FAQ for more context before reaching out.

What Happens Next

What to Expect After You Reach Out

We want the process of enquiring to be as straightforward and pressure-free as possible. Here is what you can expect after you submit your message:

  • A personal response Your enquiry will be read and responded to by a real person, not an automated system. We take the time to understand your situation before replying.
  • An honest assessment We will tell you whether we believe our services are a good fit for your situation. If we do not think we can help, we will say so clearly and, where possible, suggest alternative resources or approaches.
  • A clear recommendation If we believe we can help, we will recommend the specific service or combination of services that makes the most sense for your needs, along with a clear explanation of why.
  • No pressure to commit The initial exchange is a conversation, not a sales pitch. You are under no obligation to proceed, and we will respect your decision whatever it may be.
  • A specific quote before any work begins If you decide to move forward, we will provide a clear, detailed quote outlining exactly what is included and what the cost will be. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges.

A Few Important Notes

Before You Write

  • We do not offer free consultations or sample analyses. The initial email exchange is free and without obligation, but a full discoverability analysis or strategy engagement is a paid professional service. We will discuss costs transparently before any work begins.
  • We do not accept every enquiry. If we believe our services are not the right fit for your situation, we will tell you honestly rather than taking on work that would not serve you well.
  • We communicate by email. All consultations and correspondence are conducted in writing. We do not currently offer phone calls or video meetings as part of our standard service delivery.
  • Your information is handled with care. Any details you share with us during the enquiry process will be treated confidentially. For full details, please review our Privacy Policy.
