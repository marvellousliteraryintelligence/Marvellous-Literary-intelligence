Book Marketing Implementation

Hands-on support to turn your strategy into action. We help you execute the specific tasks that will make the greatest difference to your book's discoverability, from refining retailer pages to writing compelling copy and setting up targeted campaigns.

Bridging the Gap Between Plan and Action

A well-crafted strategy is only as valuable as the action it produces. Many authors find themselves in a familiar position: they understand what needs to change, they have a clear set of recommendations in front of them, and yet the actual work of making those changes feels daunting, confusing, or simply overwhelming alongside everything else they are managing.

This is not a failure of motivation. Implementing book marketing changes often requires navigating complex retailer dashboards, writing in a specific style that differs from your natural authorial voice, making technical decisions about categories and keywords, or setting up promotional campaigns with unfamiliar tools. These are specialised tasks, and it is entirely reasonable to want support with them.

Book Marketing Implementation is designed for exactly this situation. We work alongside you to execute the specific recommendations from your strategy, handling the detailed work that can be difficult to manage alone while keeping you fully informed and in control of every decision.

The Problem

Why Implementation Stalls

The transition from planning to doing is where many book marketing efforts quietly collapse. The reasons are varied and entirely understandable:

  • Technical complexity: Retailer dashboards, advertising platforms, and metadata systems are not always intuitive. A single misclick or misunderstood field can undermine an otherwise sound change.
  • Copywriting challenges: Writing a book description that sells is a fundamentally different skill from writing the book itself. Many excellent authors struggle to describe their own work in the compressed, persuasive format that retail pages demand.
  • Decision fatigue: When a strategy identifies multiple areas for improvement, deciding where to start and in what order can feel paralysing, especially when you are already balancing writing, life, and possibly a day job.
  • Uncertainty about impact: Without experience in book marketing, it is difficult to know which changes are likely to make a meaningful difference and which are low-priority refinements. This uncertainty can lead to either inaction or scattered effort.
  • Time pressure: Implementation takes time, and most authors would rather spend that time writing. The tension between creating new work and promoting existing work is one of the most persistent challenges in an author's career.

Book Marketing Implementation addresses these obstacles directly. We take on the detailed execution work so you can focus on what you do best: writing books that deserve to be found.

Scope

What Implementation Support Includes

The scope of each implementation engagement is shaped by the specific recommendations from your strategy or discoverability analysis. We do not offer a fixed menu of tasks. Instead, we focus on the changes that will have the greatest impact on your book's visibility. Common areas of implementation support include:

Retailer page optimisation

Rewriting and restructuring your book descriptions, subtitles, and series information on major retail platforms to better communicate genre, tone, and appeal to your target readers. This includes ensuring that your metadata aligns with the search terms your audience actually uses.

Category and keyword refinement

Researching and recommending more effective category placements and keyword configurations based on the competitive landscape of your genre. Where possible, we guide you through the process of updating these fields within your retailer dashboards.

Author profile updates

Refining your author biographies, profile images, and background information across retail and social platforms to create a more coherent and professional presence that supports book discovery.

Promotional copywriting

Writing marketing copy for specific campaigns, including newsletter announcements, social media promotions, advertising text, and retailer promotional materials. All copy is written to reflect your voice and your book's unique appeal.

Campaign setup guidance

Providing step-by-step guidance for setting up promotional campaigns on platforms such as Amazon Advertising, BookBub, or other relevant channels. We help you understand the options, set appropriate parameters, and avoid common pitfalls.

Launch coordination support

For authors preparing a new release, we can help coordinate the various elements of a launch, including timing, retailer setup, promotional sequencing, and pre-order strategy.

Important note: Implementation support is most effective when it follows a prior diagnostic or strategic engagement. If you have not yet completed a Discoverability Intelligence analysis or a Marketing Strategy, we may recommend starting there first to ensure the implementation work is focused on the right priorities.

Deliverables

What You Will Receive

The specific deliverables for each implementation engagement depend on the scope of work agreed during the initial consultation. However, you can generally expect the following:

  • Agreed scope of work: a clear written outline of the specific tasks we will undertake, the order in which we will tackle them, and what you will need to provide or approve at each stage
  • Completed copy and content: any book descriptions, author biographies, promotional copy, or campaign text we write on your behalf, delivered for your review and approval before anything goes live
  • Implementation documentation: a record of the changes made, including before-and-after details where relevant, so you have a clear reference for future updates
  • Guidance notes: written instructions for any tasks that require your direct involvement, such as updating fields within your own retailer accounts or approving changes on platforms we cannot access on your behalf
  • Completion summary: a final overview of all work completed, along with any remaining recommendations for future action

You retain full approval authority over every change. Nothing is published, submitted, or modified without your explicit consent.

Our Process

How Implementation Works

Implementation is a collaborative process. We do the detailed work, but you remain in control of every decision.

  1. Scope agreement We begin by reviewing your existing strategy or discoverability findings and agreeing on a specific set of implementation tasks. You will know exactly what we will do, what you will need to provide, and what the expected outcomes are before any work begins.
  2. Research and preparation Before making any changes, we conduct the necessary research to ensure our recommendations are well-informed. This may include competitive analysis, keyword research, or a review of current retailer guidelines and best practices.
  3. Drafting and review We draft all copy, metadata changes, and campaign materials and submit them to you for review. You have the opportunity to request revisions, ask questions, and approve each element before it is implemented.
  4. Execution Once you have approved the materials, we proceed with implementation. For changes that require access to your accounts, we provide detailed step-by-step instructions or guide you through the process in real time, depending on your preference.
  5. Review and handover After all agreed tasks are complete, we provide a summary of the changes made and any remaining recommendations. We also discuss whether ongoing support would be beneficial as you monitor the impact of the changes over time.
Who This Is For

Is This Service Right for You?

Book Marketing Implementation is designed for authors who have a clear sense of what needs to change and want experienced support to make those changes happen. It is particularly relevant if you recognise yourself in any of the following situations:

  • You have completed a discoverability analysis or marketing strategy and are ready to act on the recommendations but feel unsure about executing them yourself
  • You struggle with writing marketing copy and want professionally crafted descriptions, biographies, or promotional materials that accurately represent your book
  • You find retailer dashboards and advertising platforms confusing and want guidance to ensure changes are made correctly
  • You are preparing for a book launch and want support coordinating the various marketing elements involved
  • You have limited time and want to delegate the detailed implementation work so you can focus on writing
  • You have tried implementing changes yourself but are not confident they were done correctly or effectively

This service is available to authors at any stage of their career and with any publishing model. Whether you are self-published, hybrid-published, or traditionally published, we can tailor the implementation support to your specific circumstances and the level of control you have over your book's retail presence.

Important Limitations

What This Service Does Not Do

Clear boundaries protect both you and us. Before you enquire about implementation support, please consider the following limitations.

No guaranteed results

Implementing well-informed changes may improve your book's visibility and appeal, but we cannot guarantee specific outcomes in terms of sales, rankings, review counts, or search positions. The retail environment is influenced by many factors beyond our control.

No account access required or assumed

We do not require your login credentials for any retailer or advertising platform. For changes that must be made within your accounts, we provide detailed instructions or guide you through the process. You maintain full control of your accounts at all times.

No design or production services

Implementation support covers text-based changes, metadata, copywriting, and campaign guidance. We do not design book covers, format manuscripts, produce audiobooks, or create visual assets. If your strategy identifies design issues, we can discuss how to address them separately.

No ad spend management

While we can help you set up and optimise advertising campaigns, we do not manage your advertising budget or make spending decisions on your behalf. All financial commitments remain entirely under your control.
