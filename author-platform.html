Service 03

Author Platform Intelligence

An honest assessment of your author presence across the web, social media, and retail platforms. We evaluate how effectively your platform supports book discovery and outline practical, realistic steps to strengthen your visibility over time.

Your Platform Is Part of the Discovery Picture

When readers encounter your book for the first time, they often do more than glance at the cover and description. Many will click on your author name. They will search for you online. They will look at your social media profiles, your website if you have one, and any other traces of your presence they can find.

What they discover in those moments shapes their perception of you as an author. A coherent, professional presence can reinforce the credibility of your book. A fragmented or absent presence can introduce doubt, even if the book itself is excellent.

Author Platform Intelligence examines this broader picture. We look beyond your individual book listings to assess the full landscape of your online visibility as an author. The goal is not to pressure you into becoming a full-time content creator. It is to help you understand what your current platform communicates to potential readers and where targeted improvements could make a meaningful difference.

The Problem

The Platform Advice That Overwhelms Authors

If you have spent any time researching book marketing, you have almost certainly encountered the phrase "build your platform." The advice that typically follows is sweeping and relentless: post daily on multiple social media channels, start a blog, launch a podcast, grow an email list to tens of thousands, engage in every online community, create video content, and maintain a polished website with a regularly updated news section.

For most authors, this advice is not just impractical. It is counterproductive. The time and energy required to maintain a sprawling online presence is time and energy taken away from writing, which is the activity that ultimately produces the books readers want to discover.

The result is a cycle of guilt and exhaustion. Authors feel they should be doing more online, attempt to do more, burn out, retreat, and then feel guilty again. Meanwhile, the actual state of their platform and its real impact on book discovery remains unexamined.

We take a different approach. Rather than prescribing a maximalist platform strategy, we assess what you already have, identify what is actually working, and recommend a focused set of improvements that align with your available time, your comfort level, and your specific goals as an author.

Our perspective: A small, well-maintained platform that accurately represents you and your work is far more valuable than a large, neglected one that sends mixed signals to potential readers. Quality and consistency matter more than volume and frequency.

Scope

What the Assessment Covers

Author Platform Intelligence examines the publicly visible elements of your online presence as they relate to book discovery and reader perception. The assessment covers the following areas:

Author retail profiles

We review your author pages on major retail platforms such as Amazon Author Central, Goodreads, and any other retailer-specific profiles. We assess completeness, consistency, and how effectively these pages connect your books to your broader identity as an author.

Personal website or blog

If you have a personal website, we evaluate its structure, content, navigation, and how well it serves the needs of a reader who has just discovered your book and wants to learn more about you. If you do not have a website, we discuss whether one would be beneficial in your specific situation.

Social media presence

We examine your active social media profiles with a focus on how they present you as an author to someone encountering your work for the first time. We are not evaluating your follower counts or engagement rates as measures of success. We are looking at coherence, clarity, and genre alignment.

Search visibility

We test what appears when a potential reader searches for your author name or book title in standard search engines. This helps us understand the first impression a curious reader is likely to form before they ever reach a retail page.

Cross-platform consistency

We check whether your author name, biography, book information, and visual branding are consistent across the various platforms where readers might encounter you. Inconsistencies can create confusion and undermine credibility.

What this service does not cover

This assessment focuses on the public-facing aspects of your platform. It does not include an analysis of private analytics data, email list performance, or advertising metrics unless you choose to share that information voluntarily. It also does not include content creation, social media management, or website development.

Deliverables

What You Will Receive

At the conclusion of the assessment, you will receive a detailed written report that includes:

  • Platform overview: a comprehensive summary of your current online presence as an author, including all publicly visible profiles, pages, and search results
  • First-impression analysis: an evaluation of what a new reader is likely to experience when they search for you, click on your author name, or visit your profiles for the first time
  • Consistency audit: a detailed check of name, biography, book listings, and visual branding across all platforms, with specific notes on any discrepancies
  • Strengths and gaps: an honest identification of what is already working well in your platform and where the most significant opportunities for improvement exist
  • Prioritised recommendations: a focused list of specific, manageable steps you can take to strengthen your platform, ordered by likely impact and effort required
  • Platform sustainability guidance: practical advice on how to maintain an effective author presence within the time and energy you realistically have available, without falling into the trap of unsustainable content schedules

The report is designed to be actionable and proportionate. We will not recommend a ten-point social media strategy if your situation calls for three well-executed changes to your existing profiles.

Our Process

How the Assessment Works

The Author Platform Intelligence process is straightforward and respectful of your time.

  1. Information gathering We begin by asking you to share the links to your existing online profiles, website, social media accounts, and any other platforms where you maintain an author presence. We also ask about your goals, your available time, and your comfort level with various online activities.
  2. Public-facing audit We systematically review each element of your platform from the perspective of a reader encountering your work for the first time. This includes retail profiles, social media pages, website content, and search engine results.
  3. Consistency and coherence review We compare your presence across platforms to identify inconsistencies in naming, biography, branding, and book information that may be confusing potential readers or diluting your visibility.
  4. Report and recommendations We compile our findings into a clear, detailed report with prioritised recommendations. The report focuses on changes that are practical, proportionate, and likely to have the greatest impact on how readers perceive and discover you.
  5. Follow-up support After delivering the report, we remain available to answer questions and help you decide which recommendations to tackle first. If you want hands-on support implementing the changes, our Implementation service can help.
Who This Is For

Is This Service Right for You?

Author Platform Intelligence is designed for authors who want a clear-eyed assessment of their online presence without being told to overhaul their entire life. It is particularly relevant if any of the following apply to you:

  • You have an online presence but are unsure whether it is actually helping your book reach readers
  • You feel overwhelmed by platform-building advice and want to know what genuinely matters versus what is noise
  • You have recently published your first book and want to establish a professional author presence from the outset
  • You have been publishing for some time but your online profiles have grown haphazardly and may contain inconsistencies
  • You are considering investing time in a new platform or channel and want an informed perspective on whether it is worthwhile for your specific situation
  • You prefer a minimal online presence and want to ensure that what you do maintain is working as effectively as possible

This service is suitable for authors at any career stage and with any level of online activity. Whether you have a single social media account or a multi-platform presence, the assessment will be tailored to your actual situation.

Important Limitations

What This Service Does Not Do

We want to be transparent about the boundaries of this service so you can make an informed decision about whether it is right for you.

No guaranteed audience growth

Strengthening your platform may improve how readers perceive you and how easily they find you, but we cannot guarantee specific increases in followers, subscribers, website traffic, or book sales as a result.

No content creation or management

This service provides assessment and recommendations. We do not write your social media posts, manage your accounts, build your website, or create content on your behalf. For hands-on support, see our Implementation service.

No private analytics review

Unless you voluntarily share access to your private analytics dashboards, our assessment is based on publicly visible information. We can still provide valuable insights from public data, but the picture will be more complete if you choose to share additional context.

No one-size-fits-all prescription

We will not tell you that you must be on every platform or post at a specific frequency. Our recommendations are shaped by your goals, your genre, your available time, and your personal preferences. A platform strategy that you will not sustain is not a useful strategy.
