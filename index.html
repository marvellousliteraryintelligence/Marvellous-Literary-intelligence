The Visibility Problem Nobody Explains

Millions of books compete for attention in the online marketplace. Many good books remain invisible, not because they lack quality, but because the path between the book and the reader who might value it has gaps that no one has investigated.

Books Get Published, Then Disappear

A book can be well-written, well-designed, and professionally edited, and still struggle to reach readers. Publication alone does not create discoverability.

The Cause Is Rarely Obvious

Low visibility is usually the result of several connected issues: listing presentation, metadata, positioning, reader targeting, or platform gaps. Fixing one without understanding the others can waste time and money.

Generic Advice Does Not Work for Every Book

Every book has its own audience, competition, and marketplace context. What works for one title in one genre may be irrelevant for another. Discoverability deserves investigation, not assumptions.

Why I Started Investigating

I grew up around books from a very young age. One thing that has always bothered me is seeing good books get lost among millions of titles competing for attention on Amazon and across the online book marketplace.

That frustration made me ask a question that shaped everything I do now: Why does this keep happening, and why do the readers who might value a book sometimes never discover it?

I decided to investigate the problem instead of accepting it. That became the foundation of Marvellous Literary Intelligence.

For the past 10 years, I have helped authors address low or limited online visibility, improve how their books are presented and discovered online, and create better opportunities to reach readers.

A Research-Led Process

I do not start with assumptions about what is wrong. I start with investigation. Every recommendation is grounded in evidence gathered from your book's actual marketplace context.

Listen

You tell me about your book, your goals, and the challenges you have experienced so far.

Investigate

I research your book's listing, positioning, competition, reader landscape, and online presence.

Analyse

I identify what may be limiting discoverability and where the strongest opportunities may exist.

Recommend

You receive prioritised, practical findings so you can decide what to act on first.
