Privacy Policy

How Marvellous Literary Intelligence handles your information when you visit this website or get in touch.

Last updated: 2025. This policy applies to the website marvellousliteraryintelligence.com and all related pages.

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Marvellous Literary Intelligence website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this site or contact us, how that information is used, and what choices you have regarding your data.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with any part of it, please discontinue use of the site.

2. Information We Collect

This website is a static informational site. It does not contain contact forms, user accounts, login systems, or e-commerce functionality. As a result, the amount of personal data collected through the site itself is minimal.

2.1 Information You Choose to Provide

When you click a "mailto" link on this website, your default email application opens and you may choose to send us a message. Any information you include in that email (your name, email address, book details, questions, or any other content) is provided voluntarily by you.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or behavioural tracking tools. However, the web hosting provider that serves this site may log basic technical information such as IP addresses, browser types, referring URLs, and timestamps as part of standard server operations. This data is managed by the hosting provider and is not actively reviewed or used by Marvellous Literary Intelligence for marketing or profiling purposes.

Please note: If third-party tools (such as analytics or cookie consent platforms) are added to this website in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes and, where required, your consent will be sought.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any personal information you share via email is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry about book discovery or marketing strategy services
  • To provide the specific information or assistance you have requested
  • To communicate with you about a potential or active working relationship, if you choose to engage our services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Emails you send are stored within the email service provider used by Marvellous Literary Intelligence. Reasonable precautions are taken to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is entirely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or stored by us.

We retain your correspondence only for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry or fulfil the purpose for which it was provided. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms such as TikTok and X. These third-party sites operate under their own privacy policies, which are independent of this one. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any external site you visit. Marvellous Literary Intelligence is not responsible for the content or data practices of third-party websites.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. These may include:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided at the bottom of this page. We will respond to legitimate requests within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

This website and its services are intended for adult authors, publishers, and publishing professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When updates are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Jurisdiction and Legal Review

Important: Privacy laws vary by country and region. This policy has been written in good faith to provide transparency about our data practices, but it may not address every requirement of every jurisdiction. If you operate in a region with specific data protection regulations (such as the GDPR in the European Union, the CCPA in California, or similar legislation), you may wish to seek independent legal advice regarding how this policy aligns with your local requirements. This page may benefit from review by a qualified legal professional in your jurisdiction.

10. Disclaimer Regarding Services

While this is primarily a privacy document, it is worth noting that any recommendations, strategies, or insights provided by Marvellous Literary Intelligence are based on analysis and professional experience. They do not guarantee specific outcomes such as increased sales, improved search rankings, media coverage, or bestseller status. Results depend on many factors beyond our control, including market conditions, reader behaviour, and the actions taken by authors and publishers.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please get in touch:

Email: MarvellousLiteraryIntelligence@gmail.com

We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries promptly.
