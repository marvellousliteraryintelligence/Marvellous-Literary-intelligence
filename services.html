Our Services

Strategic Support for Authors Who Want to Be Found

Every service begins with the same question: why is this book not reaching the readers it deserves? From there, we build a clear, honest path forward.

A Different Kind of Book Marketing

Most book marketing advice starts with tactics: run this ad, post that content, build a funnel. But tactics without diagnosis often lead to wasted effort and quiet disappointment.

Our approach is different. We begin by examining the discoverability landscape around your book. We look at what readers actually see when they search, browse, and scroll. We identify the specific gaps between your book and the audience it was written for. Only then do we recommend a course of action.

The result is not a generic marketing plan. It is a focused strategy shaped by the realities of your genre, your book's positioning, and the competitive environment it exists within.

How It Works

How the Services Connect

There is no single correct starting point. Your entry into our services depends on where you are now and what you most need to understand. That said, many authors find the following sequence helpful:

  • 1. Diagnose. Begin with Discoverability Intelligence to understand the specific reasons your book is not being found. This creates the factual foundation for everything that follows.
  • 2. Plan. Use those findings to shape a Book Marketing Strategy that addresses your actual visibility gaps rather than generic industry assumptions.
  • 3. Strengthen. If your author platform needs attention, Author Platform Intelligence helps you understand what to prioritise and what can wait.
  • 4. Execute. Move into Implementation when you are ready to act on the strategy with focused, informed support.
  • 5. Sustain. Transition to Ongoing Support to maintain momentum, adapt to changes, and continue refining your approach over time.

Every author's situation is different. We will always recommend the path that makes the most sense for you, even if that means starting with just one service.

Our Commitment

What You Can Expect from Us

We believe in honest, clear communication about what strategic support can and cannot do. Here is what we commit to in every engagement:

Honest assessment. We will tell you what we genuinely see, even when the findings are uncomfortable. Flattering an author does not help their book.

Evidence-based recommendations. Our suggestions are grounded in observable data and industry knowledge, not trends or guesswork.

Clear scope. You will always know exactly what is included, what is not, and what outcomes are realistic before any work begins.

No false promises. We do not guarantee sales figures, bestseller rankings, review counts, or media coverage. No ethical strategist can, and anyone who does should be treated with caution.

Respect for your work. Your book matters to you, and we treat it with the seriousness it deserves. Our role is to help it find the readers it was written for.
