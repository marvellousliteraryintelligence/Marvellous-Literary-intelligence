Book Marketing Strategy

A structured marketing plan built around the specific realities of your book, your genre, and your current position in the marketplace. Not a template. Not a checklist of generic tactics. A strategy shaped by what is actually true about your situation.

Why Generic Marketing Plans Fail Authors

The book marketing industry is full of advice that sounds reasonable on the surface: build an email list, run Amazon ads, post on social media, get reviews, pitch to bloggers. None of this is inherently wrong. The problem is that it is offered as a universal prescription, applied in the same order and with the same emphasis regardless of the book, the author, or the market.

When an author follows a generic plan and sees little result, the natural conclusion is that they did something wrong, or that their book is not good enough. In most cases, neither is true. The plan simply was not built for their specific circumstances.

Book Marketing Strategy starts from a different premise. Before recommending any tactic, we examine the context in which your book exists. What does the competitive landscape look like? Where are the realistic opportunities for visibility? What resources and strengths do you already have? What constraints are you working within?

The result is a strategy that makes sense for your book, not someone else's.

The Problem

Tactics Without Diagnosis Lead to Wasted Effort

Most authors who come to us have already tried some form of marketing. They have posted on social media, perhaps run a few ads, maybe reached out to reviewers. The effort was real. The results were not.

The frustration that follows is one of the most common experiences in independent publishing, and it deserves to be taken seriously. When you invest time, energy, and often money into promoting your book and see no meaningful change, it is natural to feel discouraged.

What we often find when we examine these situations is that the tactics themselves were not necessarily flawed. The issue was that they were applied without a clear understanding of the underlying problem. Running ads for a book whose metadata is misaligned will not fix the metadata problem. Building a social media following will not help if the book's retail page is not converting browsers into readers. Pitching to reviewers will not move the needle if the book is categorised in a way that confuses its target audience.

A strategy is the layer that sits above tactics. It answers the question of why you are doing something before you decide what to do. Without that layer, even well-executed tactics can produce disappointing results.

A note on sequencing: Many authors find it helpful to begin with our Book Discoverability Intelligence service before moving into strategy. Understanding the specific visibility gaps around your book creates a stronger foundation for the strategic decisions that follow. However, this is not a requirement. We can build a strategy based on the information you provide during our initial consultation.

Scope

What the Strategy Covers

Book Marketing Strategy is a planning and advisory service. It produces a detailed written strategy document that addresses the following areas in the context of your specific book and situation:

Market and genre positioning

An assessment of where your book sits within its genre landscape, including the expectations of your target readers, the competitive environment, and the realistic opportunities available to you at your current stage.

Visibility and discoverability priorities

A clear identification of the most impactful areas to focus on first. This may include metadata optimisation, category adjustments, keyword alignment, or retail page improvements, depending on what the initial assessment reveals.

Channel and tactic recommendations

Specific guidance on which marketing channels and tactics are most likely to be effective for your book, and which are unlikely to justify the investment of your time or budget. We explain the reasoning behind each recommendation so you can make informed decisions.

Resource alignment

An honest conversation about what is realistic given your available time, budget, skills, and comfort level. A strategy that requires resources you do not have is not a useful strategy.

Sequencing and prioritisation

A suggested order of operations that helps you focus your energy on the changes most likely to make a difference first, rather than trying to do everything at once.

What the strategy does not include

The strategy document is a plan, not an execution. It tells you what to do and why, but it does not include the hands-on work of implementing those recommendations. If you need support with execution, our Book Marketing Implementation service is designed for that purpose.

Deliverables

What You Will Receive

At the conclusion of the strategy engagement, you will receive a comprehensive written document that includes:

  • Situation summary: a clear overview of your book's current position in the marketplace, based on the information gathered during our consultation and any prior discoverability analysis
  • Target reader profile: a description of the readers most likely to connect with your book, including how they search, browse, and make purchasing decisions
  • Competitive context: an outline of how comparable books in your genre are positioned and marketed, and what that means for your own strategy
  • Strategic priorities: a ranked list of the most important areas to address, with clear explanations of why each priority matters
  • Channel and tactic guidance: specific recommendations for marketing channels and activities, including what to focus on, what to defer, and what to avoid
  • Implementation roadmap: a suggested sequence of actions to help you move from planning to execution in a manageable, structured way
  • Measurement guidance: advice on how to assess whether your efforts are working, using indicators that are meaningful and accessible to you as an author

The document is written to be practical and actionable. You should be able to read it and understand exactly what to do next, even if you have no prior marketing experience.

Our Process

How We Build Your Strategy

Every strategy is developed through a collaborative process that ensures the final document reflects your actual situation, not a set of assumptions.

  1. Discovery consultation We begin with a detailed written exchange to understand your book, your publishing history, your goals, your previous marketing efforts, and the challenges you are currently facing. The more context you can share, the more precisely we can tailor the strategy.
  2. Market and genre research We examine the competitive landscape of your genre, including how comparable books are positioned, what readers in your category respond to, and where the realistic opportunities for visibility exist.
  3. Diagnostic review If you have completed our Discoverability Intelligence service, we incorporate those findings directly. If not, we conduct a focused review of your book's public-facing retail presence to identify the most significant visibility factors.
  4. Strategy development We synthesise the gathered information into a coherent strategy document, making specific recommendations grounded in your book's unique situation and your available resources.
  5. Delivery and follow-up The completed strategy document is delivered to you via email. We remain available to answer questions and clarify any aspect of the recommendations so you can move forward with confidence.
Who This Is For

Is This Service Right for You?

Book Marketing Strategy is designed for authors who want a clear, informed plan of action rather than a collection of disconnected tactics. It is particularly relevant if you recognise yourself in any of the following situations:

  • You have tried various marketing activities but lack a coherent framework connecting them
  • You are overwhelmed by the volume of conflicting marketing advice available online and want guidance tailored to your specific book
  • You are preparing for a new book launch and want to ensure your marketing efforts are focused on the right priorities from the start
  • You have a limited marketing budget and need to make sure every pound or dollar is directed toward the most impactful activities
  • You have completed a discoverability analysis and are ready to translate those findings into a broader marketing plan
  • You are a traditionally published author who wants to supplement your publisher's marketing efforts with an independent, author-focused strategy

This service is suitable for authors at any stage of their career, from first-time self-publishers to experienced authors navigating a changing market. The strategy is built around where you are now, not where someone else thinks you should be.

Important Limitations

What This Service Does Not Do

We want you to have a clear understanding of what a strategy engagement involves before you enquire. The following limitations are not weaknesses in the service. They are honest boundaries that protect you from unrealistic expectations.

No guaranteed outcomes

A strategy is a plan of action, not a promise of results. Implementing the recommendations may improve your book's visibility and reach, but we cannot guarantee specific sales figures, ranking positions, review counts, or media coverage.

No implementation included

The strategy document tells you what to do and why. The actual work of executing those recommendations, such as updating metadata, creating content, or setting up campaigns, is a separate step. See our Implementation service for hands-on support.

No ongoing management

The strategy is delivered as a standalone document. If you want continued guidance as you implement the plan and as market conditions change, our Ongoing Marketing Support service provides that continuity.

Based on available information

The quality of the strategy depends in part on the accuracy and completeness of the information you provide during the consultation. We work with what we know, and we will flag areas where additional data would strengthen the recommendations.
